REFERENCE IMAGE SETUP

@Image1 — REPLACEMENT MALE
- Use one clear identity.
- A multi-angle character sheet works best.
- Show the exact outfit, accessories, hair, and facial hair wanted in the result.
- If the image contains multiple panels, every panel must show the same person.

@Image2 — REPLACEMENT FEMALE
- Use a clear, full-body or three-quarter reference when possible.
- Show the exact outfit, hair, jewelry, and styling wanted in the result.
- Avoid extra people, cropped faces, heavy filters, or conflicting outfits.

@Video1 — MOTION REFERENCE
- Use a clip you created, licensed, or have permission to use.
- The video controls the shot order, camera movement, timing, environment, and physical motion.
- A clean 4–15 second clip is easiest to test.
- Do not use the RichMadeIt demo MP4 as your motion reference.

FINAL CHECK
- Image1 and Image2 are different people with clearly assigned roles.
- The source clip contains the correct number of people.
- The requested duration and aspect ratio match the clip.
- The prompt says whether the scene is silent, spoken, or lip-synced.
