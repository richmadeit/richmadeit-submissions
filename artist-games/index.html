BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few good questions.

Is the $25 session an extra charge?

The session costs $25 on its own. If you purchase a $199 launch game, that $25 is credited toward the game total. Your remaining game payments are $75 to complete the $100 deposit, then $99 before delivery.

Do I need an idea before the session?

No. Bring your song, your artist artwork, and a little about your audience. We’ll use the 20 minutes to choose one practical concept from the available game styles.

What is included in the $199 game?

One existing game style customized with your artist name, supplied artwork, and one song; a shareable browser link; one revision; and 30 days of hosting. New gameplay, extra characters, and ongoing support are quoted separately.

When will my game be ready?

We confirm your timeline before your build deposit is taken. Timing depends on the agreed customization and when your song and artwork are ready.

Can fans hear my song while they play?

The launch package lets fans hear your supplied song during gameplay. Music-platform integrations are scoped separately; counted streams depend on the platform’s rules and the tested integration.

What should I bring, and how do we meet?

We meet by phone. Bring a song link, your artwork, your artist name, and any release plans. Calendly collects your phone number, confirms your time, and takes the $25 payment through Stripe.
