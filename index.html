A FEW THINGS BEFORE WE START

Good questions.

Is the whole video $5?

No. $5 buys a separate 15-second watermarked preview. Finished videos cost $1 per second, with a 30-second minimum. A 30-second video costs $30; if you start with the preview, it is $5 plus $30 if you continue.

Do I need my song and photos ready?

Have a photo and your song ready when you send your materials. We’ll confirm the upload steps with your order. Existing customers can return to their upload page using their order reference.

Can you make a gospel, R&B or hip-hop video?

Yes. Your music guides the mood, wardrobe, setting and performance. Tell us who sings each part, especially for a choir, duo or featured artist.

What if there is more than one artist?

Duos and groups are welcome. Each package includes one artist. Tell us how many performers you want and we will quote the additional cost before production. The $5 preview is separate from the agreed video price.

When will I receive it?

We confirm the schedule after reviewing your song, references and concept. For preview-first orders, full production follows preview approval and payment of the video price.

Can AI match me perfectly?

Clear reference photos help us preserve your look. AI likeness and lip-sync can vary; your preview lets you review the direction before buying the finished video. One minor correction is included.
