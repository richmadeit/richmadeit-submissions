A FEW THINGS BEFORE WE START

Good questions.

Is the whole video $5?

No. $5 is your deposit and counts toward the total. Packages are $50 for 30 seconds, $90 for one minute and $250 for three minutes. You review a 15-second watermarked preview before paying the remaining balance.

Do I need my song and photos ready?

Not to pay the deposit. Send them on the upload page after checkout, or return later using your order reference. We start the preview once your materials are complete.

Can you make a gospel, R&B or hip-hop video?

Yes. Your music guides the mood, wardrobe, setting and performance. Tell us who sings each part, especially for a choir, duo or featured artist.

What if there is more than one artist?

Duos and groups are welcome. Each package includes one artist. Tell us how many performers you want and we will quote the additional cost before production. Your deposit counts toward the agreed total.

When will I receive it?

We confirm the schedule after reviewing your song, references and concept. Full production follows preview approval and balance payment.

Can AI match me perfectly?

Clear reference photos help us preserve your look. AI likeness and lip-sync can vary; your preview lets you review the direction before committing to the balance. One minor correction is included.
